HOW TO READ THIS

A simulation,
not a sales pitch.

  • Virtual money only.No wallet, private key, custody or blockchain transaction exists anywhere in this system.
  • Pool-model marks.Values use public aggregator observations and an adverse constant-product approximation. They are not executable quotes.
  • Momentum exits.Positions are re-evaluated against actual successive 60s, 180s and 300s observations. HOLD and SELL evidence is recorded.
  • No hidden losses.Losing trades, failed entries and costs remain in the public record. The starting €100 is the benchmark.
